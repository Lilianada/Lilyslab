Create the world you want, in your own little corner of the internet — your _Minispace_.

A place for:

- Rabbit holes, Links, and Backlinks
- Templates and Inspiration
- Tutorials   
- Posts, Essays, and Articles
- Community 
- Simplicity and Joy 

---

#### **What is Minispace?**

Minispace is a platform that encourages you to explore the web and build your own digital home — a space for your thoughts, ideas, experiments, and identity.  
It’s not about building big. It’s about building meaningfully.

We believe in small, personal websites that speak to who you are.  
We want to fuel your imagination with the best tools, resources, and inspirations to help you create something that feels _authentically yours_.

---

#### **Why should you have your own space online?**

The better question is: _why not?_

The internet gives everyone a shot at presence — a chance to build a tiny world without asking for permission.  
A space that reflects you: your stories, ideas, hobbies, half-baked thoughts, ongoing projects, or the strange little things you love.

You can use Minispace to:

- Share essays, zines, notes, or blog posts.
- Save links, bookmarks, and digital ephemera.
- Build simple sites, subdomains, and mini apps.
- Discover others doing the same.

And if you’re not sure where to begin — that’s what Minispace is for.  
To help you start. To help you _stay curious_.

---

#### **Templates & Inspiration**

You don’t have to start from scratch.  
Copy site designs you love — remix, rebuild, personalize.  
It’s encouraged. In fact, it’s part of the culture.

We share templates and encourage the community to:

- Modify font families, sizes, and colors.
- Add or remove pages or sections.  
- Rename pages or restructure the layout. 

When you adapt a design, write a short reflection about what inspired you. Link back to the original and — if you feel like it — email the original creator with a link to your version. It’s a great way to start conversations.

---

#### **Projects & Subdomains**

- **write.minispace.dev** — A minimalist space to publish essays or notes. Contribute and your work could appear on our Discover page.
- **cluster.minispace.dev** — A digital morgue / inspiration file. Save things you love, weird links, references — like your own personal wipe file. 

---

#### **How do we inspire?**

The internet is a playground for ideas. A place to build, learn, connect, and wander.

Every time you discover a personal site, dig through its pages, and read what someone wrote in their own voice — you experience a bit of magic. It’s the joy of stumbling on something real.

That’s what we want to recreate.

Minispace is here to help you:

- Learn by exploring.
- Build without pressure.
- Create without constraint.
- Share without optimizing for performance.
    

---

#### **Our Philosophy**

- Small sites > massive platforms
    
- Personal expression > performance metrics
    
- Joyful writing > algorithmic content
    
- Minimalism, simplicity, and creative freedom
    
- Everyone deserves a home on the web
    

We’re not here to compete. We’re here to exist — quietly, creatively, meaningfully.

---

#### **Not a techie? That’s fine.**

You don’t need to know code to start.  
You can learn slowly, copy from others, remix what you see, refine it until it feels like yours.

That’s the spirit of the web we love.
