

MOVEMENT & DIRECTION

Run awkwardly - stumble, lurch
Move suddenly - jerk, jolt
Step back quickly - recoil, retreat 
Enter boldly - stride in, march in 
Leave quietly - slip away, sneak out 
Drive recklessly - speed, swerve

SPEECH & COMMUNICATION

Say proudly - declare, boast 
Speak harshly - snap, scold 
Laugh softly - chuckle, snicker 
Whisper urgently - hiss, murmur
Ask carefully - inquire, probe
Reply casually - shrug off, respond 
Announce loudly - proclaim, blare

EMOTION & REACTION

Cry angrily - sob, wail
Smile broadly - beam, grin 
Frown deeply - scowl, glare
Laugh nervously - titter, giggle
Look angrily - glare
Shiver slightly - tremble, quiver
Jump suddenly - flinch, start

THOUGHT & FOCUS

Think quickly - react, decide
Guess randomly - speculate, estimate
Look briefly - glance, peek
Forget completely - blank, block out 
Notice gradually - realize, register 
Listen distractedly - tune out, drift off 
Remember suddenly - recall, flash back

Throw lightly - toss, lob
