---
title: "Write replaces Terminal, and whole book covers"
date: "2026-11-15"
version: "1.15.0"
type: feature
category: new feature
---

Terminal Notes has become Write: a quiet page for writing. Headings, bold, lists and quotes appear as you type them in Markdown. The drawer in the top corner keeps every note you've written, with a + New button and a delete icon on each one. Everything stays in your own browser and is never sent anywhere.

The bar at the bottom fades in when you hover over it. It has light and dark mode, three fonts, the text size, a button to show or hide the title, fullscreen, a Markdown cheat sheet, download as .md, a 15 minute writing timer, clear all, and a word count.

Book covers on the bookshelf now show in full instead of being cropped at the edges.

Later the same day: Write lost its Prev/Next links and the footer (the breadcrumb at the top leads back to the playground), and the bottom bar and notes button now stay put while a long note scrolls.
