The garden got a lot sillier.

- **Grow a flower:** water one on the [homepage](/) until it blooms, then plant it in your own patch.
- **[Petal Catch](/playground/petal-catch):** catch the petals, dodge the bees, beat your best score.
- **[Doodle Pad](/playground/doodle):** draw something and stick it on your own fridge.
- **[Color Theory](/playground/color-theory) and [Animation Lab](/playground/animation-lab):** two toys that replace the old "Coming Soon" cards.
- **Wander:** the dice button in the top bar sends you somewhere random.
- **A new 404 page:** getting lost should have its upsides.

There are also a few secrets hidden around the site. I'm not telling you where.
