Essays and notes can now carry an "AI-edited" tag, shown next to the title on the post and in the lists. It means I wrote the piece and used AI to edit it, not write it. Add `aiEdited: true` to a post's frontmatter to show it.

Webmentions are fixed. The site was pointing other sites to the wrong address (with "www"), so nothing could arrive. Each essay and note now shows the likes, reposts and replies it gets from around the web. The AMA page fetches mentions through the site's own server, so the webmention.io key no longer goes to visitors' browsers.

In Write Online, list items have more room between them, and empty bullets disappear when you move away from them.

Also new: four blogs in the webroll (Advik, Manton Reece, Steph Ango, yesterends) and three bookmarks about making your own website.
