Essays, notes and projects each have a "Hide locked" button that hides locked drafts and confidential projects from the list. The choice is remembered on your device, so it applies on all three pages.

The Future Topics list on the notes page lost the topics I've now written about: 50 Things I Know, Why I Read, and Why Start a Digital Garden.

Also new: three bookmarks (two on using notebooks creatively, plus Rachel Kwon's "The internet used to be* fun"), Fumadocs and Present & Correct in the misc webroll, and seven personal sites in the webroll (nikiv.dev, Mathieu De Raedt, Kwon.nyc, Naz Hamid, nikhil.io, Rach Smith, Chris Glass).
