Every essay and note now ends with its connections, all worked out from the writing itself: **Backlinks** (posts that link here with [[double brackets]]), **Mentioned in** (posts that name it without linking), and **Related** (posts sharing its tags and topic). Each list shows three posts and folds the rest behind "N more", so it stays short on phones. To pin a post to Related by hand, add `related: [title or file name]` to the front matter.

[[Double bracket]] links are forgiving now: [[indie web]], [[Indie-Web]] and [[indieweb]] all find indieweb.md, plurals and small typos still land, and words from Word of the Day work too ([[portmanteau]] opens that word's card). [[target|label]] links to one thing while showing other text. They're real links now, so they open in new tabs and search engines can follow them.

For search engines: every essay and note has a description (its excerpt, or its opening lines), its own canonical address, article share cards and schema.org data naming me as the author. Pages that had no title or description now have them, the structured data is in the HTML from the start, the sitemap includes the garden's topics, and the control room is kept out of search. Fixed three dead links on the homepage, including "Reach out", which now goes to /hello.

Links across the site get the same quiet hover: a thin, faded underline in the link's own colour.

New words: buttress and premonition, and portmanteau is filled in. New bookmarks from a11ce and Kening Zhu.
