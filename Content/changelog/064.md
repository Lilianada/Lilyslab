title: "Footnotes preview on hover"
date: "2026-11-18"
version: "1.19.1"
type: improvement
category: garden
---

Footnotes now work like Wikipedia's references. The marker is a tiny bracketed number, like [1], and hovering it (or tabbing to it) shows the note in a small card, so you don't lose your place. Clicking still jumps to the notes at the bottom of the post. On a phone, the first tap shows the note and a second tap jumps to it.
