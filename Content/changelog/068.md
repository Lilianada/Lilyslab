The Digital Garden page is a map of topics instead of a list of sections. Essays and notes sit together in eight topics (Writing, Creativity, Gardens & note-taking, The personal web, Work & projects, Mind & self, Books & stories, Life & opinions), and each topic has its own page with a short introduction. The topic introductions are placeholders for now.

Each post shows how grown it is: seedling, budding or evergreen. A "Recently tended" list shows what I've worked on lately.

The essays and notes pages open grouped by topic, with a "By date" switch for the old order. Your choice is remembered.

Every essay and note now ends with "Linked from" (the posts that link to it), a few related posts, and the topic it's planted in. These replace the previous and next links.
