Animation Lab has left the playground and Burn CD took its spot. It's for making a mix CD, start to finish:

- Paste a Spotify, YouTube or YouTube Music playlist to get the tracklist (names and lengths only).
- Add your own MP3s (or M4A, WAV, FLAC). They're matched to the playlist by their tags and file names, and you can pick files by hand for anything it misses.
- Drag tracks into order and edit their names. A meter at the top shows whether it all fits on an 80-minute CD.
- Design the front cover and the back insert (with spines and the tracklist): four layouts, four fonts, colour palettes and your own photo.
- Download a folder that's ready to burn: the songs converted to CD audio, the artwork at print size, a playlist, a cue sheet with CD-Text, and step-by-step burning instructions.

It all happens in your browser, so your music never gets uploaded.

Write is now called Write Online. The font button changes the whole page, paragraphs are a little bigger (16px) with more room between lines, and the time is gone from the bottom bar.
