The webroll sidebar has a search box now. Type a site's name, a word from its note, part of its address or a tag, and the list narrows as you type. It works with the categories too.

Color Theory has left the playground and a Transcriber took its spot: paste a YouTube, Instagram, TikTok, Pinterest or X video link and get its words back as text. It runs on paid minutes, so for now it's just for me, but you can peek.
