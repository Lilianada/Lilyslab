I wanted to know how many new people find the garden, so the site now counts first visits.

- Your browser remembers it has been counted, so coming back doesn't count again.
- All I see are totals: how many people, which site sent them, which page they landed on, and their country.
- No accounts, no IP addresses, and nothing that can tell one visitor from another.

Behind the scenes, I also got a small dashboard for answering questions and tending the garden.
