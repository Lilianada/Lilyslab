title: "A Much Lighter Garden"
date: "2026-11-04"
version: "1.5.0"
type: improvement
category: performance
---

The site now loads far less before you can read anything.

- Most pages load about 115KB of JavaScript, down from about 530KB. The homepage went from 535KB to 132KB.
- The homepage is pre-rendered, so it no longer fetches about 300KB of data every time it loads.
- Music only downloads when you press play.
- Essay images went from 22MB to 3.6MB, and the favicon from 446KB to 9KB.
- Fonts moved to WOFF2, and essays and static data are now built ahead of time.
- Removed about 35 unused packages and dozens of unused files.
