### A Book Review on a Book I Found On Pinterest

I enjoy reading books, Nigerian Non-fictions have become a fav. Contrary to that, I found this book on one of my casual Pinterest browsing. The cover looked catchy and so did the title. I was in a phase where I really didn’t know what I was doing, so a book talking about being an Adult? Well, hell yeah.


### Review

On a scale of 1 to 5, I give it a 3. This book is so personal to the writer. She spoke about her life, what she thought adulting would be like for her and what the reality was. Honestly, this was how it was for a lot of us. Imagine wanting to grow up fast and leave your parents roof to become Miss Independent only for you to leave and want to go back so bad but pride won’t let you.
