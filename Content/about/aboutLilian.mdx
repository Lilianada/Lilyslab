# About

Hi! I’m **Lilian Ada Okeke**, a passionate technologist, digital gardener, and lifelong learner. I love exploring product management, creative coding, frontend design, and sharing what I learn with others. This site is my digital home for projects, notes, experiments, and inspiration. Welcome!

## This site
Here’s a complete overview of what you’ll find here, organized by section:

### Digital Garden
- **Drafts**: Unfinished notes, ideas, and explorations. A space for sharing work in progress and planting seeds for future projects.
- **Bookmarks**: Curated links, resources, and inspirations I’ve collected from around the web.
- **Bucket List**: My goals, aspirations, and target companies I’d love to work with.
- **Bookshelf**: Books I’m currently reading, have read, or plan to read.
- **Notes**: A growing collection of thoughts, learnings, and documentation on topics I’m passionate about.
- **Catalog**: Index of content, resources, or references for creative technologists and knowledge workers.

### Writing
- **Writing**: My main blog index, featuring articles, essays, and deep dives.
- **Individual Posts**: Each writing has its own page for focused reading.

### Workshop
- **Projects**: Highlights of my professional experience, personal projects, and collaborations.
- **Logs**: Project logs and progress tracking.
- **Changelog**: Updates and improvements to the site and my work.
- **Resources**: Useful tools, guides, and references.
- **Tools**: Software, utilities, and workflows I use or recommend.
- **Shop**: Digital or physical products, recommendations, or affiliate links.

### Playground
- **App Dissection**: In-depth breakdowns of interesting apps, their UI/UX, and technical details.
- **Calculator**: Mini-apps and interactive tools.
- **Note Widgets**: Experiments with note-taking and productivity widgets.

### Other Pages
- **AMA**: Ask Me Anything! Submit questions and I’ll answer them publicly.
- **Stack**: All the hardware and software I use.
- **Now**: What I’m currently focused on.
- **Colophon**: The tools, technologies, and inspirations behind this website.
- **Welcome**: Introductory page for new visitors.
- **About**: This page—my story, mission, and how to get in touch.
- **Contact**: How to reach me (see below).
- **Changelog**: Site changes and updates.

This site is built as a digital garden—always evolving, never truly finished.
