I found this questionnaire during one of my many web explorations and decided to give it a go.
Questionnaire by [Mouseling.net](https://mouseling.net/100webmaster)

1. Please introduce yourself.

*Hi there, my name is Lilian, you can call me Lily. I'm a 26 year old Nigerian baby girl living and working in the F.C.T.*

2. How long have you been making websites?

*Started making websites in 2017 so a little over 9 years now.*

3. And what got you into the hobby?

*I won't call making websites a hobby. Although it started as such, it became a career part for me. Even now that I'm not writing code for money, I still won't term it a "hobby". But I got into writing code for financial security.*

4. What kind of website are you most interested in?

*I love building fun personal projects like my website and others you can find in my [/projects]. I also like building for B2C.*

5. What's your workflow? Do you plan your websites out thoroughly or do you come up with the design as you go along?

*Most times I plan but not thoroughly. When I have an idea for what I want, I use pen and paper or Canva to design what it should look like.*

6. Please link to your biggest inspirations.

*[Colophon](/colophon)*

7. What's your favourite part about making websites?

*Interacting with the finished work.*

8. And the thing you struggle with the most?

*Backend work.*

9. Do you keep the same layout on all of your pages? Or do you use different ones?

*I try to keep the same layout but I'm not strict with that . Layout changes depending on what I aim to achieve.*

10. How confident are you with CSS?

*Very confident.*

11. Do you know how to correctly use `<dl>`?

*Yes. `<dl>` is a description list: `<dt>` holds the term and one or more `<dd>`s hold its description. It's for name and value pairs such as glossaries, metadata and FAQs, not for general layout.*

12. What is your favourite HTML element?

*`<details>` and `<summary>`. You get a working open and close toggle with no JavaScript at all.*

13. If you're making a new web page from scratch, what is the first thing you do?

*Set up the skeleton: the doctype, `lang`, the charset and viewport meta tags and a `<title>`, then the semantic landmarks (`<header>`, `<main>`, `<footer>`) before any styling.*

14. Do you know JavaScript?

*Yes. The site is written in TypeScript (typed JavaScript) with React and Next.js.*

15. How about PHP?

*No. Nothing on this site uses PHP; the server side is Node.js through Next.js.*

16. Does your website have a theme that you stick to?

*Yes, it does.*

17. Are you more focused on content or design?

*Can I say both?*

18. Do you own a domain name? If not, would you ever want to?

*Yes, I own a couple of them.*

19. What do you think of nostalgia-focused or "retro" websites?

*I think they're fun to explore.*

20. Is your HTML valid? Do you even check?

*Mostly. The pages are generated by React and Next.js, which catch most structural mistakes, but I don't run every page through a validator.*

21. What are your opinions on buttons and banners?

*No opinions.*

22. What do you think of button walls in particular?

*No thoughts.*

23. If you started over again, would you make something similar or completely different?

*Similar.*

24. Are you envious of other people's websites?

*Not envious, but in awe.*

25. What text editor do you use?

*VS Code.*

26. Why do you use that one?

*It's pretty easy and I'm used to it. I've tried others but I still fell back to VSCode.*

27. Do you host your image files on your web server, or on another host?

*Both. Most of my own images live on my server in the `public` folder, while some older posts still point at images hosted elsewhere (Pinterest, Unsplash).*

28. This might not be relevant to you, but what's your opinion on the Neocities vs. Nekoweb debate?

*Not until you mentined it, I've never heard or seen anything on Neokooweb. I think that answers the question.*

29. How much server space would you estimate your main website takes up?

*About 135 MB, most of it images: roughly 62 MB in `public` and 73 MB in the content folder.*

30. Do you keep local backups of your files?

*Yes. Everything is in Git, so there's a copy on my laptop and another on GitHub, and every deploy can be rolled back.*

31. Do you prefer simple or highly visual websites?

*Simple websites.*

32. Do you stick to certain colours? Do you do that on purpose, or is it your subconscious?

*I do stick to certain colors on purpose. I like to keep this cohesive.*

33. Have you ever thought about quitting? Why?

*I think about quiting a lot. I have quit jobs too. When an experience begins to steal my piece and give me anxiety or cause me mental stress, I don't hesitate to leave.*

34. Do you have many webmaster friends, or is it a solitary hobby?

*I don't have any friends. It's a solitary hobby. I am open to making friends though.*

35. Do people in your real life know about your website?

*Yes, a couple of my friends know that I own a website but I doubt anyone of them visits it at all.*

36. Do you update your website very often? How often is "very often"?

*I update my website when I can. I keep track of books I read on a monthly basis via my [/bookshelf](/bookshelf) page. This helps me to update the website on a monthly basis.*

37. And the overall design, do you change that much? Why or why not?

*My website design is ever changing. Nothing is finished. If i wake up with  a new idea for how I want a page to look, I'm changing it immediately.*

38. Is your website more you-focused, hobby-focused, or outside world-focused?

*I want to believe it's more me and my hobbies focused.*

39. Do you do web design professionally?

*Once upon a time, I used to. I'm still open to it.*

40. If not, would you like to? And if you're comfortable answering, what do you do for work?

*I am open to it and would absolutely love to come back. Currently I run [littlescribbs.co](https://www.littlescribbs.co).*

41. Do you communicate with people by email very much?

*I do when I can and if necessary.*

42. Some people reject social media and use websites as a replacement. Do you keep social media outside of your website?

*Yes I do. My business runs online mostly.*

43. How about instant messengers? Do you use a mainstream one like Discord or Telegram? Or something like Matrix? Do you avoid them?

*I do try to avoid them. I've tried them but they never stuck.*

44. Do you listen to music while you work on websites? If so, what kinds of artists?

*I listen to pop and afrobeats.*

45. Do you keep everything you make on one website, or do you have more than one?

*I have more than one. [Lilianada.com](https://www.Lilianada.com)*

46. On a similar note, do you keep to one topic on your site, or many?

*Many topics.*

47. Do you present your real self, or at least try? Or do you construct a persona on purpose?

*I present my real self. I present the self I aim to become.*

48. Have you ever made a good friend thanks to your website?

*I have met and interacted to a good few people but never really made any friends.*

49. Are you happy with the way HTML and CSS currently work?

*Mostly yes. Modern CSS (grid, flexbox, custom properties, container queries, `:has()`) covers almost everything I used to need JavaScript or hacks for.*

50. What are practices that you think people should avoid?

*Divs used as buttons, images without alt text, text in low-contrast colours, layout shifts from images with no dimensions, and loading a whole framework for a static page.*

51. What about under-utilised practices, or things you think people should do more?

*Semantic HTML, RSS feeds, alt text, and plain old links between personal sites.*

52. Do you use a lot of semantic HTML? Or are you guilty of generic structure?

*Mostly semantic: headers, mains, navs, articles, ordered lists and real buttons. Some generic divs still sneak in for layout.*

53. Do you consider different browsers?

*Yes. I stick to features with broad browser support and avoid anything only one browser has.*

54. Speaking of, what's your preferred browser? Convince your readers why they should use it.

*Chrome browser. The arsenal of extensions and the seamlessness.*

55. And what OS are you on?

*Sequoia 15.6 (24G84)*

56. Do you have a strong opinion on that, or do you just happen to use it?

*I just happen to use it.*

57. Are your websites mobile-friendly?

*Yes. The layout is responsive, the sidebar collapses on small screens and I check pages at phone width.*

58. What are your thoughts on autoplay?

*Against it. Nothing on this site autoplays sound, and animations respect reduced-motion settings.*

59. What are your thoughts on webrings? Are you in any?

*Yes, I'm in Meta Ring, IndieWeb, Bucketfish Webring, CSS JOY, Webmaster Webring and GeekRing. I think they're cool. It's a nice way to meet new people and get found by others.*

60. Do you have any web shrines? What do you like to see in that sort of page?

*Don't think I do.*

61. Are your websites "cliche", in your opinion?

*Nothung cliche here.*

62. What is your ideal website? Are you striving for that, or for something else?

*My ideal website is my current website.*

63. Are you an artist? Do you draw or design your own assets?

*Maybe I am but not in those ways.*

64. What are your favourite resource sites?

*Pinterest, [Blogroll](https://blogroll.org/), [indieweb.org](https://indieweb.org/directory) Cosmos, and many that I can't recall.*

65. Is there a habit you just can't get away from no matter how hard you try?

*I don't think I'm trying to get away from any habit.*

66. What's your biggest advice for a new webmaster?

*It takes time to get to "awesome". Enjoy the process and stay open to change.*

67. Do you keep all your styling in CSS? Or do you hard-code some?

*Almost all of it is in CSS, written as Tailwind utility classes plus a global stylesheet. Inline styles only show up in a few older Markdown posts.*

68. What do you think of frameset layouts?

*They're a piece of web history, but they break bookmarking, the back button and accessibility, so I wouldn't use them today.*

69. How about table-based layouts?

*Tables are for tabular data. CSS grid and flexbox do layout better and keep it accessible.*

70. Do you subscribe to the ideas of "one-column", "two-column" and "three-column" layouts? Do you use any of these?

*Yes, loosely. The site is a two-column layout (sidebar plus content), and the reading pages are a single centred column.*

71. Do you spend longer on the HTML or the CSS?

*The CSS, by far. The HTML structure comes quickly; the spacing, type and dark mode take the time.*

72. Have you ever made a page with no CSS? It's useful for your thoughts.

*Not as a finished page, but I check how pages read without styles. Good HTML should still make sense with no CSS.*

73. Do you ever find yourself making layouts with nothing to put on them? Or do you only make layouts when the need arises?

*Mostly when the need arises.*

74. Would you consider yourself a beginner? Or advanced? Somewhere in the middle?

*Somewhere in the middle.*

75. Do you have a habit of looking at the source code of websites you visit?

*If it intrigues me enough and I want something from it, I do take a look.*

76. How did YOU learn how to make websites?

*I learnt by doing.*

77. Do you ever force elements to do things they're not supposed to?

*Sometimes. A few custom components fill gaps that native elements don't cover, but I try to stick with the right element and style it.*

78. Thoughts on floating elements?

*Floats are fine for wrapping text around an image. For layout, flexbox and grid replaced them.*

79. When you're sizing stuff, what do you use first? Do you use px, em, %, or something else?

*Rem for type and spacing (through Tailwind's scale), % and viewport units for widths, and px for borders and fine details.*

80. Do you have a favourite font?

*I do like Geist and Nitti for monospace.*

81. Would you run a website with another person? How would that work?

*I don't know how that would work but I'd love that.*

82. Do you surf the Web to find new personal websites very often?

*100%. I live for it.*

83. Do you bookmark other people's websites? How would you feel knowing someone else bookmarked yours?

*I bookmark a lot of websites. My list is ever growing. I'd be elated to find out that someone loved it enough to bookmark it.*

84. What do you want people to be most impressed with when they see your website?

*I want them to be impressed by the organization, simplicity and all the fun corners waiting to be explored.*

85. Are you interested in technology outside of websites? Do you collect?

*Not really.*

86. How often and for how long are you online?

*I live online unless when sleeping or taking a walk.*

87. When it comes to your website, who is your target audience?

*Everyone who owns a personal website and genzs.*

88. Have you ever been interested in XHTML?

*Not really. HTML5 replaced it.*

89. Do you program in general? Have you ever written a program for use with or on your website, not counting simple JavaScript?

*Yes. Besides the site itself I've built tools that run on it: a writing app, an audio transcriber, a mix-CD planner, and scripts that create content and add bookmarks.*

90. Speaking of programs that help you make websites, what do you think of static site generators (SSGs)? Have you ever used one?

*Yes. They're great for personal sites. This site works much like one: pages are built from Markdown files with Next.js.*

91. Do you keep a hitcounter? Why or why not?

*Sort of. There's a small first-visit counter that counts each browser only once and stores no personal data, rather than a public hit counter.*

92. Do you frequent forums? Which ones?

*discourse.32bit.cafe*

93. Do you write your page content directly into the editor, or do you prepare it elsewhere, like a text document or a Word document?

*I write them in my notes app first or my web editor [notes.lilyslab.xyz](notes.lilyslab.xyz) before writing in Markdown files in my editor, then commit them to the site.*

94. Do you think you appear cool to others? A more accurate answer now: do other people ever say you're cool?

*I don't know. Never heard that one.*

95. Are you embarrassed of your old work? Have you ever deleted everything out of shame?

*Never embarrased. How then will I see the progress that I've made over the years?*

96. Would you close down your website if you couldn't update it, or would you leave an archive?

*I'll leave an archive.*

97. Do you reveal a lot about yourself on your website? Or are you more secretive?

*I reveal a lot. Maybe I shouldn't? Nah who cares. I don't get that much people reading what I write on here.*

98. Are you willing to reveal who your best online friend is, and/or if they have a website?

*I got none.*

99. And do you optimise the images on your website?

*Partly. Images in the site's components go through Next.js image optimisation, which resizes them, serves modern formats and lazy-loads them. Images inside older Markdown posts are served as they are.*

100. We're out of time! How do you feel after answering 100 questions? ...other than exhausted.
*Glad to be part of something.*
