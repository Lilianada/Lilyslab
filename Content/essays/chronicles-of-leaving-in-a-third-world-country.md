It’s a disaster. The efforts you put into your dream job or business and you still aren’t able to afford basic things not to talk more of luxuries. 

You’ll see your counterparts in developing countries put in the same effort and they succeed, grow and make it. It’s devastating when you realize that it’ll take double or triple the effort to be able to afford luxury.
