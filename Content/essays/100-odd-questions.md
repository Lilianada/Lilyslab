100 Odd Questions To Have Fun Answering by [samsolarsystem](https://samsolarsystem.neocities.org/100OddQuestions)

1. Would you rather never need to sleep again or never need to eat again?

2. If your brain was a place, what would it look like?

3. When playing two truths and a lie, what are your three go-to facts?

4. What is a topic you could talk about for hours?

5. Which songs do you normally gravitate towards when doing karaoke?

6. What is a word that you wish existed in your language but doesn't?

7. If you had to work for one of Batman's villains, who would be the least horrible boss?

8. What was your favourite Flash game when you were a kid?

9. How do you rate things? 0–5 stars, 1–10 points, a secret third option? Do you use fractions or decimals?

10. What is the coolest flag?

11. What's the best Halloween costume you've ever pulled off?

12. If Kirby ate you, what ability would he get?

13. How many hours do you have on your most-played game?

14. If you were in a fighting game, what would your character's gimmick be? Intro animation? Special moves?

15. What is the loudest you have ever been in a movie theatre?

16. When travelling, do you unpack your things and get comfortable, or do you keep everything inside your suitcase and only take stuff out when you need to?

17. If you were in a band, what kind of music would you make?

18. If you were tasked with creating a new Monster High character, what monster would they be designed after?

19. If you were a demon, which three items would people need to summon you?

20. If you were an actor, would you prefer to be known for one role or multiple roles?

21. Who is the most relatable fictional character ever?

22. Without considering rent prices, what would your ideal home look like?

23. What is the real-life equivalent of -1 HP (assuming your max HP is 100)?

24. Do you believe in aliens? How do you think they would look?

25. If you could pet any animal without any risk of being attacked in the process, which one would you choose?

26. What is your favourite onomatopoeia?

27. What is something you enjoy doing despite being terrible at it?

28. Imagine you see a bowl full of candy, but you have no idea what flavour each one is. Which colour would you pick?

29. What is your favourite fun fact?

30. How do you celebrate your birthday?

31. Imagine you're on a train or bus that's almost full, but you've somehow acquired a perfect seat with nobody sitting next to you. What would be worse: someone sitting right beside you or nobody wanting to sit next to you?

32. What is your favourite clothing item you own?

33. Is there anything you expected to be a frequent thing in your life because of fiction, but were really disappointed to find out only happens in movies?

34. What's your go-to pose for pictures?

35. If you got lost in a mall, where would your friends look for you first?

36. How would you describe your perfect day?

37. Do you think you could pass a middle school maths exam right now without studying?

38. If you were a fictional character with a distinct theme song that played in the background every time you entered a scene, what song would it be?

39. Do you like your initials?

40. Do you collect anything?

41. Do you wear accessories? How often?

42. Have you ever grown attached to something that was supposed to be a placeholder?

43. If you were invited to a potluck, what would you bring?

44. What socks are you wearing right now?

45. How often do you take pictures of yourself?

46. Where is your favourite place to hang out?

47. If you died and became a ghost, who, where or what would you haunt for the rest of eternity?

48. What is something we deem normal but is actually pretty weird when you think about it?

49. What is your most useless skill?

50. How often do you swear?

51. What motivates you?

52. If you were famous, what would your autograph look like?

53. What is something you haven't done in a long time that you would love to try again?

54. Is there any food that you think looks really tasty but don't actually like? You just like the idea of it?

55. What do you think of piracy (as in watching movies illegally, not literal pirates)?

56. If you accidentally stole something from a store, how expensive would it have to be for you to feel guilty and go back inside to pay for it?

57. What is the best gift you have ever received?

58. Do you like keeping a schedule, or do you prefer to rawdog all your responsibilities?

59. Have you ever installed a virus on your computer?

60. What is your favourite thing to write with?

61. If you didn't have to worry about money, what would your ideal job be?

62. What's the furthest away from your home you have ever been?

63. Are birthdays a holiday?

64. Is there anything people say reminds them of you, but you don't really see the resemblance?

65. How often do you rewatch media you like?

66. What animal is your fursona?

67. If you could change one thing about yourself, what would it be?

68. If you were an enemy in a video game, what loot would you drop after you died?

69. If your life was a TV show, what would play during the intro?

70. How often do you wear your favourite shirt?

71. What kind of clothes do you wear to the airport?

72. What do you do when you find a bug in your house?

73. What is your opinion on "so bad it's good" media?

74. If you were abducted by aliens but didn't have proof, who would you tell? Would you even tell anyone or take it to the grave?

75. If you met someone with a personality identical to yours, would the two of you get along?

76. If you were stuck in a time loop, how long would it take you to start doing batshit insane stuff that you would never do otherwise?

77. If you magically lost the ability to feel embarrassment, would your life be better or worse?

78. How do you draw yourself? (Bonus points if you add an image.)

79. What is your favourite storytelling medium?

80. What do you think is your most noticeable feature?

81. Imagine you're a character from a cartoon who wears the same clothes every episode. What would those clothes be? Would you have different outfits for special occasions, like a beach episode or a Christmas special, or would you wear the same thing all season?

82. Who do you main in Mario Kart? Smash Bros.? Any other video game where you get to pick a character?

83. If someone told you they were stuck in a time loop and needed your help, would you believe them?

84. What kind of bag do you carry?

85. If you were a fictional character, in what ways would the fandom mischaracterise you?

86. Do you have beef with an inanimate object?

87. Are you a stereotype?

88. What's the weirdest place you have ever slept?

89. Do you have a catchphrase?

90. What is a non-edible item that you think would be really tasty if you could eat it?

91. What is the best part of amusement parks?

92. What do you keep in your pockets?
