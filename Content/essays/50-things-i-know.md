A growing list of things I know. Updated daily as I learn new things.

1. I know that working out makes you look good. When you look good, you feel good.
2. I know that I am not a main character in any person's story. I have accepted this and do not question people's decisions and choices even when they are against me. I quickly accept it and move on. The quicker I accept it, the faster I forget they or their decisons existed.
3. I know that fitness is one place where, as long as you put in the work, you'll definitely see the results you want.
4. God's time is 100% different from man's time.
5. I know that quickly exiting situations that do not favour you will save you so much time and prevent embarrassment.
6. I'm just a girl.
7. Life doesn't always go as planned.
8. You can never be too careful.
9. I know that 'Could never be me' can very well one day be you.
10. I know that hope is delayed disappointment.
11. Aspire to make more mistakes, make no one twice.
12. I know that quantity can lead to quality when it comes to art and creativity. Create those shitty art everyday until they become good.
13. I know that life has no meaning. Once you realise this, you can give it whatever meaning you want.
14. I know that there are no good guys and bad guys in politics. There are only bad guys and worse guys.
