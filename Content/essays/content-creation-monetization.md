I got asked a question today and after answering it, I decide to share my answer to that question here so that it can help provide some of clarity to someone else.

### Question

I want to create content. I've joined some groups already. Learning Capcut, creating video with AI, etc but I'm not sure of the sure monetary route. Can you enlighten me more? I will appreciate that.

### Answer

I'm not a content creator who makes money directly from content. I use content to promote my products and generate sales. In other words, the content gives my products visibility and serves as advertising, but the actual money comes from selling physical products. It's as simple as that.

If you want to take content creation more seriously, then it depends on what your goal is. What kind of content do you want to create? The value you can offer people and how you can monetize it will depend on the direction you choose.

For example, do you want to explore AI video creation? There are lots of courses on that. I'm not particularly interested in making AI videos, but I've seen people online who claim to be making money from it. If you understand how to create and edit the videos, know which platforms to publish them on, and understand how to grow your audience, there's an opportunity there. YouTube, for instance, is one platform you could explore.

Another option is entertainment content. If your goal is simply to entertain people online, you can start by picking up your camera, making videos you think people will enjoy, jumping on viral trends and sharing them on platforms like TikTok and YouTube. You can eventually monetise your content through platforms like TikTok, YouTube and its Shorts feature.

But if you want to create content around the work you already do, I think there's an even better opportunity for you.

For example, I think the visa assistance services you offer are a very good way to make money through content creation. All you need to do is open a YouTube channel or Tiktok account, confirm the current monetization requirements for Nigerian accounts (open a foreign account if you can), and start creating videos about visa applications.

Talk about the different types of visas, how to apply, the requirements, common mistakes people make, the challenges involved and other useful information. At the end of each video, include your contact details so that people who need professional assistance can reach you.

My younger brother does something similar. He offers services online, helping people with certain applications and certifications. I can't go into the details of exactly what he does, but he uses YouTube to attract customers.

He creates videos teaching people how to complete some of these processes themselves. However, not everyone wants to do the work independently. Some people would rather pay someone to handle it for them. That's where his services come in.

I think you could apply the same approach to visa assistance. Create videos about visa applications, the pros and cons of different routes, common mistakes, travel requirements and everything people need to know when considering a move abroad.

There are so many people looking to relocate. If you position your channel as a resource for people who want to Japa, you could build an audience around topics like getting visas for different countries, applying for student visas, becoming a permanent resident, understanding immigration requirements and exploring relocation opportunities.

You could also take it a step further by organizing paid classes or creating groups on Telegram or WhatsApp for people interested in relocating to specific countries. For instance, you could have a paid class or community for people who want to move to the USA, where you guide them through the process, answer questions, explain requirements and help them understand the steps involved.

Essentially, you could take the kind of assistance you provided me and make it available to a much larger audience.

I think that's a very practical way to use content creation to grow a service-based business. You're not necessarily making money from the videos themselves. You're using the videos to establish credibility, reach potential customers and bring people into your business.

You get the gist.
