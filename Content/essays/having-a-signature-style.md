For close to 4 years now, I’ve had this habit of wearing a particular necklace for a long stretch, sometimes even more than a year. When I can, I stack up to three necklaces.
I currently only have one on my neck because the other two got broken at different times due to my carelessness.

My necklaces are usually a good conversation starter when I meet someone new. People like to understand why I stack a cross pendant, the body of woman and a pearl on my neck. To be honest, there’s no particular reason, I stack them because all 3 have different lengths chains. Some people think I’m catholic but the woman’s body throws them off and they ask if I’m a lesbian. This is usually so funny.

When one of my necklaces broke and I told my boyfriend at the time, he asked if it was gold while looking distraught for me. I replied “it’s not gold but I really liked it”. That was all I needed to make the decision that I needed to start investing in pure gold pieces instead of just gold plated.

### What does wearing the same necklace for over a year say about me?

It probably means I love to have a signature look. You might see that same necklace I wear all the time somewhere else and you’d first think of me. Having a signature look or piece is a sign of personal style and taste.
