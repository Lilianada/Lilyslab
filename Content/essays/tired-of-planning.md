The reason you're not executing is because you spend all the time you could use in executing to plan.

You look for inspiration, save b-rolls, plan the content, write the script, schedule the shoot day but end up not executing because you have exhausted yourself during the planning stage.

I consider myself a confused creative because at the end of the day, I get confused and overwhelmed with the amount of "inspos" I have saved and accumulated for "later".

I know I don't need more inspiration or planning. I just need to start executing. And to create as much as possible irrespective of the quality.

I now aim for quantity because I have spent so much time obsessing over quality that I ended up not creating a thing.

My goal for the rest of the year is to shoot and upload 100 videos before the year runs out.

No more planning. Tripod out, camera up, press play.

When I shoot and edit, I can figure out where to post.

Stop planning, start creating.
