"When life appears to be too complicated, disrupted, or messy, I like to visualise it on two axes: horizontal and vertical. Daily chores, duties, and trivialities, in short, everything I must do, are represented as points with numerical values along the horizontal axis. Contrary to what I must do, I see everything I love doing, which I can't quantify but can experience qualitatively, on the vertical axis (like art, spirituality, compassion, kindness, love)."
