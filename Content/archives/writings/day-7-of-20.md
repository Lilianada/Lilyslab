𝗗𝗮𝘆 𝟳/𝟮𝟬: The AI Prompt Glossary

As someone who works with multiple AI agents and LLMs, I’ve realized how hard it is to keep track of all the great prompts I use daily.

So, I created a simple site (shoutout to Cursor.learn for the design inspiration!) to store my favorite prompts – both the ones I personally use and those I find online or on X.

This AI Prompt Glossary is open to everyone. You can even submit your own prompts to be added!

Whether you're building with ChatGPT, Claude, Bard, or other LLMs, prompts are the key to unlocking their potential. This glossary is my way of organizing and sharing the knowledge I’ve gained from experimenting with these tools. I hope it helps you refine and expand your own prompting skills.
