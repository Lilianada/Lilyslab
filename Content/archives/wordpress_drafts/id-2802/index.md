 **Hourglass**

A rose I choose not to be

Pretty it is, magnificent it smells

But plumb as a running line is its figure

I choose to be the hourglass

Admired by the moving eyes

A sun I choose not to be

Glorious it is, bright it shines

But bulbous as a plant's root is its figure

I choose to be the hourglass

Envied by all gender

An amoeba I choose not to be

Having not so many fine qualities

Shapeless is its predominant feature

I choose to be the hourglass

Having the most desired figure of man
