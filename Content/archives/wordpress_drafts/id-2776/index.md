It's the weekend so we gotta be sleek. Yunno, so I learnt that not only females use tote bags. As a young boy in school, a tote bag could come in handy and any time. A Denim Tote Bag would be a bomb added accessory to your closet, don't you think. 

Making a 2 shade denim tote bag would require you to condemn two of your old denim trousers/skirt or you could simply run down to your local store and get you a few yards of 2 different shades of denim. Things you'll need;

- 2 different shades of denim
- Needle and thread or sew machine
- Tape rule
- Scissors
- Chalk

You need to be careful so you don't waste your denim material especially if you got yours out of an old pair.

- Get your material out by losing the seams holding the skirt/trousers together. I used both an old pair of trouser(remnant from a DIY'ed denim swimwear) and a skirt(it was formerly a DIY'ed frayed end short skirt)
- Now, cut 3 pieces out from each shade of denim. They must be the same measurements
- Place the different shades side by side and mark with a chalk to indicate.
- Join them together. Close the bottom and the sides
- Fold the end/hem by half inch or 1
- From any of the 2 shades get out a long string or attach some together to form a hand for the bag
- Attach them to the bag. Be careful so that your work comes out clean and attractive.

Tadaa, you now have a denim tote bag to go with that fly outfit. Do check my Instagram page to see me rocking my bag. Oh, I decided to add a little design to it instead of leaving it like that.
