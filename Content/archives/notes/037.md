Dear young Nigerian, Adopt These 7 ‘Never Give Up’ Attitudes: 

1. Remember why you started 
2. Know that failure is feedback. 
3. Focus on what needs to be done. 
4.  Feed your strength. 
5. Starve your weaknesses 
6. Commit to smart work and deep work. 
7. Be steadfast and optimistic.
