Writing for me is a way to properly process my thoughts, assimilate information and keep reference to things for future use.

It is important to me to write in a manner that would make coming back to the note easy.

I don't just write for the sake of it, I make sure that I am able to write what I understand or have understood, how I am feeling at the moment and how I felt at a particular time.

I have used a number of note taking apps but my best 2 so far are:
- Apple Notes App
- [NoteIt-Down App](https://notes.lilyslab.xyz/)

The reason I choose both is because of accessibility. Both apps open in less than 2 seconds and they both work offline, have minimal features and allow me to move(export) my notes whenever I like.
