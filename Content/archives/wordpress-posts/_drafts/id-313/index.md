How do you rock your denim short skirt.

This is a look I fell in love with. Its a playful and chic look that makes me comfortable.

This skirt was initially longer than it is now. It was also old and patched so instead of throwing it out I decided to make it into cool short skirt since I have none.

It goes with whatever top or shoe and isn't stressful to maintain

**How I Styled It**

This is a picture of the old skirt. Cut the piece to your desired length .

Fray the hem or sew it .

Make ripped patches if desired.

Very simple!

Your denim skirt is ready to go.
