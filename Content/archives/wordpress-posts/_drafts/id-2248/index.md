**What my perfect day should look like**

Wake up by 7:15am

Say a little prayer

Brush my teeth and then have my bath

Put on a little makeup and my lovely little thingys

How I love oversize clothings. Put on a track pant and a small top to go with my socks

![21fe3e2cf6ecd824611d8a1be98405be](images/21fe3e2cf6ecd824611d8a1be98405be.jpg)

Dress my hair and make my bed

Go downstairs and make breakfast of Fried potatoes and Pepper sauce

Watch an episode of "This is it" My fav naija movie

Go out with my significant other for Launch and talk about the One million things we might have discussed about

![bfb2a94ce980abad8a8d19e56aeb875d](images/bfb2a94ce980abad8a8d19e56aeb875d.jpg)

Get back home by 6:00pm, Pull off probably everything  I have on

Make dinner of Noodles, boiled egg, pepper sauce and suya

Eat it while watching another episode of "This is it" A Nigeria movie on a newly wedded couple's life

Have my bath and put on some comfy night clothes with my face mask on

Go through my phone to reply messages, comments and tweets after which I wash off the face mask

Go to my blog and draft something new for the future

Check out what's trending and what isn't

Have a little drink in between (Lacasera)

Shut down everything and go to bed

Absolutely  Stress Free and Interesting
