Today’s post is going to be about the Egusi (Melon) soup I prepared some weeks ago. Last year, preparing egusi soup used to be easy but the end product wasn't always satisfying. My first Egusi soup this year was a total success (I’m not hyping, I’m being realistic). I have to feel myself a bit for being successful. I decided to do the whole thing differently and it worked out fine.

The major problem I had with my other Egusi soups was that, the egusi (melon)  was never in clumps when I prepared it and the beauty of egusi soup is when it is in clumps (forming big lumps in the soup). But this problem has been solved. I have two methods to make it lumpy or in clumps.

As Nigerians, I assume we know the needed ingredients for the preparation of this soup but for those who do not know, they are listed below. Note; these are the important ingredients, whatever isn’t mentioned here is therefore optional.

**Ingredients**

1. 3 cups of egusi ( depending on the preferred quantity)
2. Vegetable (I prefer bitter leave or spinach)
3. Salt
4. Maggi ( I use Star maggi and Knorr, you can use the one of your choice)
5. 2 sachets of Onga seasoning
6. 2 cups of palm oil (milk tin for measurement)
7. Beef
8. Stock fish
9. Dry fish
10. Cray fish
11. Fresh pepper or Cameroon pepper
12. These are the ingredients I use, your seasonings might differ.

**Recipe**

- Wash your meat, stock fish and dry fish thoroughly
- Blend or ground the melon seed with cray fish
- Boil the meat, stock fish and dry fish (make sure to season it well)
- Pour the boiled meat and fish into a neat bowl
- Put a none sticky pot on the gas/stove and heat it
- Add your palm oil, let it heat for a minute
- Add your grounded pepper (tomato paste if required) I used a little for my soup
- Let it fry for 3 minutes
- Make sure your grounded melon is in a smooth paste (thick not watery) Do this by adding water to the powder until it is thick
- Add the stock (water from boiled meat)
- Use a spoon and add the paste into the pot
- Do not stir
- Let it boil for 10-15 minutes
- Add your meat and fishes
- Add more water if needed
- Add salt and seasoning
- Stir a little, make it in slow motion burning
- Add your desired vegetable and let it boil for another 10 minutes
- Stir properly now, add more seasoning if required

Note; You can also decide to do the following below to make the melon seeds in to clumps (this is my mum's method)

- Do not add water to the grounded melon/ melon powder
- Get a mortar and a pestle and pound the powder till it starts bringing out oil
- Then you mould it with your hands into small balls
- Add them to the boiling meat and allow to boil for 15 minutes
-  Add your oil, seasoning and salt
- Let it boil for 10 minutes before you add your vegetable

_Voila_! Your soup is ready. You can now serve it with either pounded yam, eba or semovita.
