What's up lovely people?

Some few weeks ago which was during the holidays, I was back home at my roots where I came from. I was looking through the pictures of my dad hung on the wall when I came across some of his 90s pictures. It was then I took note of the way he dressed in all the pictures. I was awed, (I bet this look got him more chicks than he imagined). We are in the 21th century and I’m sure only a few percent of the male population would still want to dress that way. But one thing about it was, it was unique and eye catching. So I decided to do a look book on the male 90s dressing. True, this look isn’t for everyone but for those who it fits, it fits well.

Not all of the 90s dressing were cool, some of them are meant to stay dead. But this look, no this look is hot. Khaki trousers or jean trousers with an oversize button up t-shirt tucked in, belt and a black or brown shoe to match. Make sure to button down a bit, that’s eye catching.
