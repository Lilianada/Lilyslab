Hello lil Birdies,

Where are all my Fashion and Style Lovers at? I got a new update for y'all.

Ruffles, Pleatings, Flare, Lantern sleeves and Bell sleeves have really been trending since the summer. Anyone who doesn't know this isn't really fashion inclined. I mean, who makes a dress now without one of these beautiful details.

These are some of the dresses I made from the summer even until recently. And honestly, it is now my signature look. It's really funny how I feel the urge to add one or two of these details to my dresses and even to that of my clients.

 

Beautiful right?

I got the inspiration of my asymmetric with pleatings from [ShopMaju's](https://www.instagram.com/shopmaju/) Instagram page and also from Pinterest. Until these trends end, I'm rocking my sleeves and ruffles and I don't care how exaggerated they might look.
