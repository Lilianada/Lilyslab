Hey there lovelies,

Today I come with a new package. I decided to write on something from our childhood and this popped up. Let's go↓

We all know the Whot card game is an indigenous Nigerian game. It has been here since the days of my grandparents and is still here.

It is an interesting and fun game to play. It's easy to learn and not too complicated. It can be played in different ways but the normal way is the major.    If you didn't play this game while growing up, then you didn't have the full experience of fun mixed with wickedness and cruelty in your childhood. I bet those who did understand me perfectly.

The thing about this game is the lessons it taught me. Playing this game, one has to be cautious,secretive, smart and predictive. It taught me that things can change for worse or better in a twinkle of an eye. Just be ready for whatever it is.

>  You're playing with your friend and you are sure of victory for yourself cause you're beating that person. Then suddenly the person gives you hold on, pick 2, then pick 3, general market, last card and check up. Then you sit there in shock trying to comprehend what just took place. It hurts so bad that you start planning a mini death for that person in your head. Like bruh, why did you have to be so cruel. If I were the one, I'd simply say 'Ojoro- cheating! Lol, even if I know its not cheating.

One's luck doesn't shine every time. Sometimes it's just funny how someone loses a game after boasting of victory. Underestimating someone's capability is another dangerous thing, you don't judge a person's skill by age or physical look, "I speak from experience". 

**Moral**\- The little things in life teaches us lessons that we fail to understand. This game has a lot of lessons attached to it. It is left for us to figure out. Experience can come from fun and interesting things, it doesn't have to be uneventful. The experiences I got from this game are lesson teaching. If I start telling you stories, you'd laugh from the beginning till the end, it was fun though.

Share with me your experiences.
