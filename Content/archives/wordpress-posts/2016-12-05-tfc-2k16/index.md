One thing about teenage girls is; they possess so many clothing and accessories and then when its time to hangout with friends or go on date, they suddenly have nothing to put on.

For the boys, they just go with whatever they find in their wardrobe, only some of them actually take their time "to look it".

I decided to put on a little photo shoot show t display the clothing that trended during the summer. Also, combining the clothes in your wardrobe to get something _slay_\-_ish_ to put on. I titled it  _**Teens Fashion Combo 2k16 (TFC 2k16)**_

So for those who have been asking what TFC is all about, this is it.\[p.s The idea is&was solely mine\]
