​It’s going to be Valentine’s day in a day's time and so I decided to make a pre val post show casing two lovely outfits any lovely dark girl could use for Val outing. 

I’ve been having some thoughts about owning velvet gowns and my thoughts have been  cleared.  I tried both of them out and I ended up looking like a really peng dark chocolate.

I got these outfits from a boutique collection I’m working with in my local area zone. They have really good feminine stuff and they are upright affordable. You should check it out sometimes.
