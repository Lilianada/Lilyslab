_Mehn!_ This Christmas isn't as fun as I thought it would be.

Kay! I just had my shower and I'm on my bed writing this little "before Christmas post" _sighs._

I don't know what a typical Christmas Eve is supposed to be like but in my house right now, there is no fun. Aside the fact that I and my family traveled back home to the east, it's so lonely here..._p.s_ the trip wasn't cool apart from the food we ate at a restaurant on our way, _damn!_ The food was good. Awaiting the arrival of other family members is really boring me.The only cool thing was, I and my brothers had a plate of "Semovita and White soup (Ofe Nsala)" that I made for the evening. God! It was hot, pepperish and delicious. Everyone's mouth was on fire except my dad's, he is used to delicacies like that, lol. Apart from the wonderful meal and the sounds of knockout or _banga_, there's nothing special about this evening. I don't know if it's just my house though. I'm guessing this has something to do with Buhari ( lmao, Nigerians blame him for almost every thing that ever goes wrong e.g someone blamed him because harmattan came late)

By next year, recipes of my favorite Nigerian dishes will be out on my blog, dishes including White soup (locally known as Off Nsala in Igbo).

_Oh well, It's gonna be Christmas in a few hours, so **Merry Christmas** lovelies. Kisses😘😘 and loves💞💞 from Ada👸_
