On a random day, I asked myself…

Why not build tools instead of just creating landing pages?

That thought led me to start building tools—not just tools I enjoyed, but ones that could make my work easier. I wanted tools that were always available, free of ads, simple, and minimalist—tools that would help me focus on my tasks without distractions.

One of the tools I built is the **𝗥𝗶𝗰𝗵 𝗧𝗲𝘅𝘁 𝘁𝗼 𝗠𝗮𝗿𝗸𝗱𝗼𝘄𝗻 𝗘𝗱𝗶𝘁𝗼𝗿**: https://lnkd.in/dmjN8DHb.

Let me explain why I built it. On my website [lilyslab.xyz](lilyslab.xyz), I have a notes page, a writings page, and several other sections. Most of my content starts in **𝗠𝗮𝗿𝗸𝗱𝗼𝘄𝗻**, written on my Obsidian board. Occasionally, I update these pages directly through my IDE—it’s quick and easy.

This tool eliminates the hassle of manually converting text to Markdown and vice versa. It even goes a step further by converting **𝗛𝗧𝗠𝗟 𝘁𝗼 𝗠𝗮𝗿𝗸𝗱𝗼𝘄𝗻** or **𝗽𝗹𝗮𝗶𝗻 𝘁𝗲𝘅𝘁**.

If you use AI agents and often copy their responses, you’ve likely faced formatting issues. This tool is perfect for converting those responses into clean, plain text.

Give it a try today!
