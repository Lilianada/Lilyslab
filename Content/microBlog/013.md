While reading 'Building a Second Brain' by Tiago Forte I came across this page that spoke about the "Twelve Favorite Problems" and I immediately got a pen and paper to jot mine down.
- How can I write better?
- What can I do to speak better?
- How can I improve the quality of my life?
- How can I keep better relationships with friends?
- How can I better manage my time to become more productive?
- How can I improve my relationship with God?
- How can I become more knowledgeable in my field?
- What can I do to make eating healthier easier?
- What can I do to get a job, keep a job, stay gainfully employed and prepared for the rainy day?
- How can I make better life decisions?
