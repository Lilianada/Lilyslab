**Meaning:**

1. A large group of musicians playing various instruments together, especially one combining string, woodwind, brass, and percussion sections.
2. The part of a theater where musicians play, typically in front of the stage.
    
**Example:** "The symphony orchestra performed Beethoven's Ninth to a sold-out audience."
