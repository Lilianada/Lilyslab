word: Mercurial  
pronunciation: /mɜːˈkjʊəriəl/  
partOfSpeech: adjective
---

**Meaning:**

1. Subject to sudden or unpredictable changes of mood or mind.
2. Volatile, sprightly, or lively in character.
3. Relating to the planet Mercury or the god Mercury.
    
**Example:** "Her mercurial temperament made her exciting but exhausting to be around."

**Similar:**
- Volatile
- Capricious
- Changeable
- Unpredictable
- Fickle
- Erratic

**Context:** "The *mercurial* nature of online discourse makes it difficult to build sustainable communities around shared values."
