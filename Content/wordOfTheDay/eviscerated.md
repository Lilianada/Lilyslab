---
date: 2025-07-02  
word: Eviscerated  
pronunciation: /ɪˈvɪsəreɪtɪd/  
partOfSpeech: adjective
---

**Meaning:**

1. Having had the internal organs removed.
2. Having been thoroughly deprived of vital content or significance.
3. Completely destroyed or devastated.
    
**Example:** "His argument was eviscerated by the opposing counsel's detailed rebuttal."

**Similar:**
- Gutted
- Disemboweled
- Hollowed out
- Demolished
- Devastated
- Destroyed

**Context:** "The critic's harsh review completely *eviscerated* the filmmaker's latest work, leaving no redeeming quality unscathed."
