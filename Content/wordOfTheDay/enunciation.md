**Meaning:**

1. The act of pronouncing words clearly and distinctly.
2. The act of expressing or stating a proposition, theory, etc., definitively or systematically.
    
**Example:** "The speaker's perfect enunciation made the lecture easy to follow despite the complex subject matter."
