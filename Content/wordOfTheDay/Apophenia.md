---
date: 2026-05-15  
word: Apophenia  
pronunciation: /ˌæpəˈfiːniə/  
partOfSpeech: noun
---

**Meaning:**
1. The tendency to perceive meaningful patterns, connections, or significance in random or unrelated data. Often associated with seeing order or intent where none objectively exists.

**Example:** "his theory relied more on apophenia than on evidence"

**Similar:**
- Pattern-seeking
- Pareidolia
- Overinterpretation
- Projection
- False association
- Illusory correlation

**Context:** Commonly discussed in psychology, philosophy, conspiracy theory analysis, and creative thinking. It can describe both harmless human pattern-recognition tendencies and more extreme interpretations of coincidences or random events.
