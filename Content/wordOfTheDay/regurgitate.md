---
date: 2025-06-25  
word: Regurgitate  
pronunciation: /rɪˈɡɜːrdʒɪteɪt/  
partOfSpeech: verb
---

**Meaning:**

1. To bring swallowed food up again to the mouth; to vomit.
2. To repeat information without understanding or thinking about it.
    
**Example:** "Students often regurgitate facts without truly comprehending their meaning."

**Similar:**
- Vomit
- Repeat
- Recite
- Echo
- Parrot
- Reproduce
