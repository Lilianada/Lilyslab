---
date: 2025-07-06
word: Trite
pronunciation: /traɪt/
partOfSpeech: adjective
---

**Meaning:** 
1. Lacking originality or freshness; dull on account of overuse.
2. So commonplace as to be obvious and boring.

**Example:** "The speech was filled with trite phrases and clichés."

**Similar:** 
- banal
- clichéd
- hackneyed
- overused
- stale
- unoriginal
- commonplace
- platitudinous
