---
date: 2025-06-29  
word: Vertiginously  
pronunciation: /vɜːˈtɪdʒɪnəsli/  
partOfSpeech: adverb
---

**Meaning:**

1. In a way that causes vertigo, dizziness, or a whirling sensation.
2. In a way suggesting extreme height, steepness, or precariousness.
    
**Example:** "The climber looked down vertiginously at the valley far below."

**Similar:**
- Dizzily
- Giddily
- Precipitously
- Steeply
- Dizzingly
- Loftily

**Context:** "The modern economy moves *vertiginously*, with fortunes made and lost at speeds that disorient our evolutionarily unprepared minds."
