---

word: Affectation
pronunciation: /ˌæfɛkˈteɪʃən/
partOfSpeech: noun
---

**Meaning:** 
1. Behavior, speech, or writing that is artificial and designed to impress.
2. A studied display of real or pretended feeling.

**Example:** "Her constant use of French phrases was just an affectation."

- pretense
- pretension
- artificiality
- insincerity
