**Meaning:**

1. To swallow greedily or in large quantities; to engulf or devour rapidly.
    
**Example:** "He seemed to ingurgitate the entire meal without pausing to breathe."

**Similar:**
- Devour
- Gulp
- Swallow
- Consume
- Engulf
- Gobble
