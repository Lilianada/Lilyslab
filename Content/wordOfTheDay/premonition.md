---
date: 2026-11-20
word: Premonition
pronunciation: /ˌpriːməˈnɪʃən/
partOfSpeech: noun
---

**Meaning:** 
1. A strong feeling that something is about to happen, especially something unpleasant.
2. A warning given in advance; a forewarning.

**Example:** "He had a premonition that the trip would go wrong, so he packed twice."

**Similar:** 
- foreboding
- presentiment
- intuition
- hunch
- forewarning
- omen
- sixth sense
- gut feeling
