---
date: 2025-07-10
word: Inextricable
pronunciation: /ˌɪnɪkˈstrɪkəbəl/
partOfSpeech: adjective
---

**Meaning:** 
1. Impossible to disentangle or separate; closely connected or intertwined.
2. So complex or entangled as to be impossible to escape from or solve.

**Example:** "The history of the two countries is inextricable from their shared border."

**Similar:** 
- inseparable
- intertwined
- entangled
- indivisible
- interwoven
- interconnected
- bound together
