word: Tandem
pronunciation: /ˈtændəm/
partOfSpeech: noun, adverb
---

**Meaning:** 
1. A bicycle with seats and pedals for two riders, one behind the other.
2. A group of two people or things working together.
3. Having two things arranged one in front of the other.

**Example:** "They worked in tandem to complete the project"

**Similar:** 
- together
- in partnership
- in conjunction
- jointly
- cooperatively
- side by side
- in collaboration

**Etymology:** 
From Latin "tandem" meaning "at length" or "finally"
