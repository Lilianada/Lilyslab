word: Oneiric

partOfSpeech: adjective

**Meaning:**

1. Relating to dreams or dreaming.
2. Having dream-like qualities; resembling a dream.
    
**Example:** "The film had an oneiric quality that blurred the line between reality and fantasy."

**Similar:**
- Dreamlike
- Surreal
- Phantasmagorical
- Ethereal
- Illusory
- Fantastical

**Context:** "The artist's paintings are known for their *oneiric* landscapes that transport viewers to strange and beautiful otherworldly realms."
