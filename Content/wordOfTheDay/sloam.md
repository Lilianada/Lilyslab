word: Sloam
pronunciation: /sloʊm/
partOfSpeech: noun
---

**Meaning:** 
1. A mixture of clay and water used in pottery; slip.
2. Fine particles of clay or mud suspended in water.

**Example:** "The potter dipped the vessel into the sloam to create a smooth finish."

**Similar:** 
- slip
- clay mixture
- mud
- slurry
- sediment
