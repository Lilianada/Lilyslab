---
date: 2025-07-10
word: Luxuriate
pronunciation: /lʌɡˈʒʊərieɪt/
partOfSpeech: verb
---

**Meaning:** 
1. To enjoy oneself in a luxurious way; to indulge in comfort or pleasure.
2. To take self-indulgent delight in something.

**Example:** "She decided to luxuriate in a hot bath after her long day at work."

- indulge
- bask
- revel

- relish
