**Meaning:** 
1. (noun) A structure of stone or brick built against a wall to support or strengthen it.
2. (noun) Something that supports or strengthens an idea, argument or person.
3. (verb) To support or strengthen something, especially an argument or a position.

**Example:** "She buttressed her argument with years of notes and examples."
