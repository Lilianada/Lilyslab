**Meaning:** A long, complicated way of expressing something. Beating about the bush. Using one too many words when fewer words can be used. Circumlocution comes from the Latin words circum, "circle," and loqui, "to speak." So circumlocution is speaking in circles, going round and round in a wordy way without ever getting to the heart of the matter.
