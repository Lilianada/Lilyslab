word: Reverie  
pronunciation: /ˈrɛvəri/  
partOfSpeech: noun  
---

**Meaning:** 
1. A state of being pleasantly lost in one's thoughts; a daydream. An imaginative or dreamy musing detached from immediate surroundings or practical concerns.  

**Example:** "she was jolted out of her reverie by the phone"  

**Similar:** 
- Daydream  
- Trance  
- Musing  
- Fantasy  
- Dreaminess  
- Contemplation  

**Context:** Often used in literature, poetry, and everyday speech to describe moments of dreamy thought, creative imagination, or reflective escape.
