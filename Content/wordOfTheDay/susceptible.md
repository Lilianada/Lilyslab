word: Susceptible

partOfSpeech: adjective

**Meaning:** 
1. Likely or liable to be influenced or harmed by a particular thing.
2. Easily affected by or sensitive to something; vulnerable.
3. Open to or admitting of (a particular interpretation or treatment).

**Example:** "Young children are particularly susceptible to peer pressure and need guidance to make good decisions."
