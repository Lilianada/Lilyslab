word: Plethora
pronunciation: /ˈplɛθ(ə)rə/
partOfSpeech: noun
---

**Meaning:** A large or excessive amount of something.

**Example:** "consumers are confused by the plethora of choices on the market"

**Similar:** 
- Excess
- Abundance
- Overabundance
- Superfluity
- Surfeit
- Profusion

**Context:** Originally a medical term referring to an excess of blood in the body, now commonly used to describe any overwhelming abundance of choices, options, or items.
