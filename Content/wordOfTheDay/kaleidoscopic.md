word: Kaleidoscopic  
pronunciation: /kəˌlaɪdəˈskɒpɪk/  
partOfSpeech: adjective
---

**Meaning:**

1. Resembling a kaleidoscope, especially in having a constantly changing pattern or sequence of elements.
2. Made up of a complex mix of elements; multicolored and continuously changing.
    
**Example:** "The festival was a kaleidoscopic display of costumes, music, and food from around the world."

**Similar:**
- Ever-changing
- Multicolored
- Variegated
- Prismatic
- Diverse
- Metamorphic

**Context:** "The *kaleidoscopic* nature of our media environment makes sustained attention increasingly difficult."
