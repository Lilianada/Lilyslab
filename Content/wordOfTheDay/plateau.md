**Meaning:** 
1. (noun) An area of relatively level high ground; a tableland.
2. (noun) A state of little or no change following a period of activity or progress.
3. (verb) To reach a state of little or no change after a period of activity.

**Example:** "After months of improvement, her performance reached a plateau."
