word: Ephemeral

**Meaning:**

1. Lasting for a very short time; fleeting or transitory.
    
**Example:** "Their digital fame was ephemeral, fading within days."

**Similar:**
- Transient
- Fleeting
- Short-lived
- Momentary
- Temporary
- Passing  

**Context:** "Digital gardens are conscious rejections of the web as a _stream_. They embrace a web which is non-ephemeral and builds value over time and with continued investment."
