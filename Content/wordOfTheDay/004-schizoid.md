**Meaning:** denoting or having a personality type characterized by emotional aloofness and solitary habits.

**Example:** Schizoid personality disorder is a condition where a person shows very little, if any, interest and ability to form relationships with other people

**Context:** "Try to be utterly schizoid about it all--using each personality a s a refuge from the other." - Philip Larkin
