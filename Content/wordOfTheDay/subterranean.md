**Meaning:**

1. Existing, occurring, or done under the earth's surface.
2. Hidden; secret; concealed; underground.
    
**Example:** "The old city was connected by a network of subterranean tunnels."

**Similar:**
- Underground
- Below-ground
- Hidden
- Concealed
- Buried
- Secret

**Context:** "Much of our digital infrastructure exists in *subterranean* cables that span continents and ocean floors."
