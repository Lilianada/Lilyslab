word: Sublunary

partOfSpeech: adjective
---

**Meaning:**

1. Situated beneath the moon; earthly or mundane.
2. Pertaining to this world as contrasted with heaven; worldly, temporal, or earthly.
    
**Example:** "The poet contrasted celestial beauty with sublunary concerns."

**Similar:**
- Terrestrial
- Earthly
- Worldly
- Mundane
- Mortal
- Temporal

**Context:** "While technology promises transcendence, we remain bound by *sublunary* constraints—our bodies, our time, our finite attention."
