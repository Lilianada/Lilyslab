word: Oscillating

partOfSpeech: verb
---

**Meaning:**

1. Moving or swinging back and forth in a regular rhythm.
2. Varying or fluctuating between different states, positions, or conditions.
    
**Example:** "Her mood was oscillating between excitement and anxiety about the upcoming event."

**Similar:**
- Fluctuating
- Wavering
- Swinging
- Alternating
- Vacillating
- Varying

**Context:** "Our modern attention is constantly *oscillating* between different digital streams, rarely settling long enough for deep thought to emerge."
