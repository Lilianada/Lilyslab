word: Comeuppance  
pronunciation: /kʌmˈʌpəns/  
partOfSpeech: noun
---

**Meaning:**

1. A punishment or fate that someone deserves, especially a retribution for wrongdoing or bad behavior.
    
**Example:** "After years of treating others poorly, he finally got his comeuppance when the board voted to remove him as CEO."

**Similar:**
- Retribution
- Punishment
- Just deserts
- Payback
- Reckoning
- Consequences

**Context:** "Watching the arrogant character in the film finally receive their *comeuppance* provides a satisfying conclusion to the narrative."
