word: Masochistic  
pronunciation: /ˌmæsəˈkɪstɪk/  
partOfSpeech: adjective
---

**Meaning:**

1. Deriving pleasure from one's own pain or humiliation.
2. Behaving in a way that indicates enjoyment of experiencing pain, suffering, or humiliation.
    
**Example:** "There's something masochistic about continuing to check social media when it only makes you feel worse."

**Similar:**
- Self-punishing
- Self-defeating
- Self-destructive
- Self-harming
- Sadistic (opposite)
- Self-tormenting

**Context:** "There's a *masochistic* element to repeatedly engaging with technologies we know diminish our well-being."
