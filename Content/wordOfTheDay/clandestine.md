---
date: 2025-07-05  
word: Clandestine  
pronunciation: /klænˈdɛstɪn/  
partOfSpeech: adjective
---

**Meaning:**

1. Kept secret or done secretively, especially because illicit or unauthorized.
2. Conducted with or marked by hidden aims or methods; covert.
    
**Example:** "The spy maintained clandestine communications with foreign agents through encrypted messages."

**Similar:**
- Secret
- Covert
- Surreptitious
- Undercover
- Furtive
- Stealthy

**Context:** "The historian uncovered evidence of *clandestine* meetings between government officials that changed the course of the negotiations."
