---
date: 2025-07-22
word: Mnemonic
pronunciation: /nɪˈmɒnɪk/
partOfSpeech: noun, adjective
---

**Meaning:** 
1. A device or technique to aid memory, especially a pattern of letters, ideas, or associations.
2. Relating to or designed to aid memory.

**Example:** "The mnemonic 'Roy G. Biv' helps remember the colors of the rainbow"

**Similar:** 
- memory aid
- reminder
- prompt
- memory device
- aide-memoire
- memory trick

**Etymology:** 
From Greek "mnēmonikos" meaning "of or relating to memory," from "mnēmōn" meaning "mindful"
