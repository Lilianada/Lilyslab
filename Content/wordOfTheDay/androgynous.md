---

word: Androgynous
pronunciation: /anˈdrɒdʒɪnəs/
partOfSpeech: adjective
---

**Meaning:** 
1. Partly male and partly female in appearance; of indeterminate sex. Identifying and/or presenting as neither distinguishably masculine nor feminine.

**Example:** "a stunningly androgynous dancer"

**Similar:** 
- Unisex
- Gender-neutral
- Ambiguous
- Hermaphroditic
- Epicene
- Non-binary

**Context:** Often used in fashion, art, and gender studies to describe appearances or expressions that blend masculine and feminine characteristics.
