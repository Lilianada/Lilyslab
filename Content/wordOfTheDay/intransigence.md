**Meaning:**

1. Refusal to compromise or agree; stubbornness or inflexibility.
    
**Example:** "The negotiations failed due to the intransigence of both parties."

**Similar:**
- Stubbornness
- Inflexibility
- Obstinacy
- Rigidity
- Unyielding
- Obduracy

**Context:** "His intellectual *intransigence* prevented him from considering alternative viewpoints, limiting the depth of his understanding."
