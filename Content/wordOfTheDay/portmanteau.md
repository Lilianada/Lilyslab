**Meaning:** 
1. Portmanteau is a word blending the sounds and combining the meanings of two others, for example motel or brunch.
2. (noun) A large travelling bag, usually made of stiff leather, that opens into two equal parts.
3. (adjective) Combining several uses or qualities in one.

**Example:** "Smog is a portmanteau of smoke and fog."
