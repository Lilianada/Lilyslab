In ala igbo (Igbo land)
A Chi is a person's representative in the spiritual realm. Chi is not Chukwu(God).
A Chi is a person's own god or spirit, guiding him through life from conception to death.

When you see a person standing, he stands not alone, but alongside his Chi, 
The Chi is the one that stands in judgement and gives the recount of a person's life lived on earth.  It is said to be androgynous. Different person can have the same Chi but not at the same time, meaning that the host of a Chi can be as many as possible but not at once. A Chi's time with a host spans for as long as the host is alive. The Chi uses its lived experiences with its former hosts to guide the life and destiny of the present host.
One can say that the Chi of a person guides his choices, destiny. It can not interfare with the decisions a man chooses to make, it can only guide, suggest or advice, the choice to make it left for the man.

Chi cannot see the future occurancies of a man.
