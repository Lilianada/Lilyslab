Bearblog is a super-fast blogging platform that puts privacy-first at its forefront. This platform is simply HTML, no JavaScripts and no stylesheets. It was created by [Herman Martinus](https://herman.bearblog.dev/) who continues to maintain it.

Some of the awesome features of bearblog includes:-
- It is lightweight (~2.7kb) and optimized for speed.
- There are no ads, no trackers or scripts.
- Pages and contents are written in markdown.
- It offers free .bearblog.dev domain and accepts custom domains too.
- It is built to last forever. Herman is so certain that bearblog will never shutdown nor get acquired.
- You get free themes.
- Amongst others, the platform is open sourced which means it is publicly available on github.
