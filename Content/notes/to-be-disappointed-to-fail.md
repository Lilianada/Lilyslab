I tried running a snail mail club, I really wanted to but I failed before I started. I sent out my first snail mail package to my first subscriber and I didn’t feel good about the package so I have decided that maybe now is not the best time to start a snail mail subscription. I have to do it because I can, I like it and enjoy the process instead of doing it because I want to be the first to start it.

I went for an Easter Bazaar market for 2 days and it was a market that thought me that when I go for markets I need to introduce products that will get people to make purchases and even if they don’t make purchases, they’ll be moved to follow us on socials.

The worst market I have been to in my 3 months of running this business. Not only did I not make any good sales, the sales made were more like pity sales from my friends who wanted to come and support me.

After a few weeks of reflection, I came to a conclusion that my target audience just wasn’t at that bazaar and so I didn’t have to feel bad in any way.
