I wanted to leave a review of this book ghost because I have been ghosted before but I just couldn't bring myself to really write about it.

Ghosting is a terrible thing to have to go through.
Ghosting someone who actually hurt you is one thing, but ghosting someone you claimed to have loved?? Most especially after professing love to them? Doing it twice? Damn!!

Nina didn't deserve to be treated the way she was treated by Max. 
As someone who's currently going through a heartbreak, I really can imagine the hurt and pain she had to endure, twice from the same person and in succession. 

And her friend Lola? She reminds me of Charlotte from "Sex and the city". Lola knew she would find love, she was hopeful and hopefully she does find it.

The book made me to really rely on my female friends at the height of my heartbreak. I don't think I've ever relied on people the way I relied on my friends. I'm grateful for them and hope we all find love, love that doesn't ghost us.

Funny story, like Nina, after my breakup, for the first time ever, I tried online dating. Come to find out these apps are used by people to promote and patronise hook-up culture. I had to leave the same week I joined. I did get one date out of it, but that ended after the second date.

Oh well, we move.
