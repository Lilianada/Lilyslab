"Winners never quit and Quitters never quit"
Do you really believe this to be true? Let me burst your bubble, the truth is Winners actually know when to quit. Not knowing when to quit is actually what breeds mediocrity and you wouldn't want to waste your time doing something you're not okay with or you have no passion for.
