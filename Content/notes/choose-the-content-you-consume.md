Replace dopamine consumption with slow content? CHOOSE the content YOU consume. 

Make your life so interesting that it’s almost impossible to engage in brain rotting activities. Instead of deleting the app, using time blocking apps or ultimately creating a limitation; engage in activities that make you forget to pick up your phone.

We use doomscrolling as a way to escape our life, escaping boredom. But imagine having a life so full, and exciting that you didn’t want to escape from it. 
Let your attention latch onto something nourishing.
