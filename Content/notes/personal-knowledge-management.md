### Digital Zettelkasten 
- It is a method for managing your knowledge
- It emphasizes connection not collection 
- Productive note taking 
- Slip-box method
- Atomic notes

This is a way smarter way of taking notes. A bit more complicated but much more manageable and efficient in the long run.
