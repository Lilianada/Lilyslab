I've been seeing the word *"Indie"* for a while now and didn't know what it meant. I decided to kill my curiousity my checking it and... well, we have another word to abuse. 

> An "indie developer" refers to an individual or small team of developers who create software (like games, apps, or other digital products) without being affiliated with a major publisher or large corporation. They typically have full creative control over their projects and work independently.
