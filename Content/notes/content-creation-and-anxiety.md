I have been creating content on Instagram for 2 years now, I randomly post on X from time to time via my web browser (I don't see why I should install it if I don't want to be there frequently), and I've been writing on LinkedIn for 6 months now.

Content creation on Instagram and LinkedIn gives me literal anxiety. You're constantly trying to beat the algorithm or at least be in its favor. You have to be consistent to get noticed while still having a full-time job, hobbies, and friends and family to engage with. It felt like a lot. It felt like pressure, and I needed that pressure off my shoulders.

I want to be loud about my skills and what I know or what I'm learning, but can I do that without worrying about Instagram algorithms, viral hooks, and trending sounds? 
Can I?
