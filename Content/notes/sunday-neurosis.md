The weekend is almost over, tomorrow is Monday and you're probably dreading getting back to work. Or maybe, you don't have a job to get back to, your work week has no work in it and Sunday is just a reminder that while everyone else has a job to get back to, you have nothing. You feel empty and without a purpose because you have nothing to look forward to.
Both the former and the later has been me in the last 12 months.
I always day dream of finding that one thing that I would look forward to getting to every Sunday. Hopefully I find it soon.
