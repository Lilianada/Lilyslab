The weekend is almost over, tomorrow is Monday and you're probably dreading getting back to work life. Or maybe, you don't have a job to get back to, your work week has no work in it and Sunday is just a reminder that while everyone else has a job to get back to, you have nothing. You feel empty and without a purpose because you have nothing to look forward to.
