It could infact, one day, be you.
The distance between 'could never be me' and 'damn, it is me' is just one bad decision, one bad day, one careless statement, one impulsive action, one stroke of misfortune. 
We're too quick to judge other people's unfortunate ordeal but no one ever plans for such things to happen.
Life will humble you so quickly you won't even see it coming.
That situation you thought would never be you because you're so clever or so smart will shockingly be you.
