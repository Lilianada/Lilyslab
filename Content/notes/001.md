Today marks the beginning of my 20-day product building challenge! After leaving She Code Africa and a brief stint at a company that wasn't the right fit, I'm channeling my energy into creating while job hunting for Associate PM roles/ Engineering Manager roles and Project Management roles.

After a hundred and twenty prompts using V0 and Cursor, I’ve finally launched LilysLab (lilyslab.xyz) - my digital workshop and tech lab where I'll showcase all 20 projects from this challenge. The dashboard features:

• Project gallery with live demos
• My building process documentation
• Resources I've created along the way
• Tech stack details for each build

Why start here? Every builder needs a workshop, and I wanted a centralized home for this journey. I built this using v0 and will be leveraging AI throughout this challenge to work smarter, not harder.

Each day, I'll be tackling a new mini-project: sometimes a PRD, sometimes a functioning app, sometimes a case study. Follow along to see what's possible when combining human creativity with AI assistance!
