The world and all within it is ephemeral. We must understand this and accept this for us to live life in the moment and live it fully[^1]. Enjoy your present as this is all that is truly yours. When you accept that everything is temporal, it will help you to be prepared for the worst without being negative.

[^1]: Read Ikigai in order to help you understand this.
