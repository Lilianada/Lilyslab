Micro.blog is an independent blogging platform founded by Manton Reece in 2017. It provides its users access to blogging at their own domain name. The platform is used for short posts, full essays, photo blogs, and email newsletters. You can see how good a platform it is for hosting your digital garden from the little we already know about it.

Some awesome features of micro.blog:-
- Micro.blog is a part of the [[fediverse]].
- You own your content
- Cross-platform posting: when you post to your blog, your posts and photos are also sent to followers on Mastodon and elsewhere.
- Multi-formating publishing:  you can use it for short posts, full essays, photo blogs, podcasts and email newsletters.
- It has no likes, no boosts or trending tags features.
- It supports platforms like Mastodon, Bluesky, Threads, Tumblr, Nostr, LinkedIn, Medium, Pixelfed, and Flickr.
