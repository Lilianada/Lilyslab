Today I decided to re-evaluate the reason I started building the Minispace.dev app. The vision isn't mine, but the idea is cool, I'm just not executing properly. So I will leave it for now and try to do something different. 
Maybe a pivot.
Let me try a fresh idea, one that would be exciting to maintain and cost effective as well. One that wouln't need marketing, one that would make me happy to work on even if people don't find it.
 
---

I wrote down a few things, but I haven't gotten the full picture yet. No need to rush it though.


**On why I bought the Minispace.dev domain:**

I found bearblog.dev,
I loved the concept,
I envied the vision,
I hated the execution.
So I decided that I would execute it better.
One month later, 
I was still struggling with a lot of things,
I gave up.
It was easy to give up
because the vision was never mine.
Then I thought to pivot instead.
I needed something that was mine
Something I would enjoy building
Something I could grow 
Something I could grow with.


**What is Minispace**

This is a platform that inspires you to explore the internet.
Get your own space in the internet.


**How do we inspire?**

The internet gave us an opportunity to find people who live hundreds and thousands of miles away just by clicking a few buttons. Everyone has the chance to create a presence, a space, an identity — to design and refine it, make it their own, give it personality, and add bits to it.

Finding these spaces, exploring them, breaking them down, reading what the writer has to say, gives me my fair share of daily dopamine.

Why should everyone have one?
The real question is, why not?

You can do whatever you want with it, add whatever you want — without constraints or restrictions. Build something you might never have in real life, or just create something good enough to share the tiny bits that make you you. Tell stories, share tales, express your thoughts.

If you want to do that and don’t know where to start... I’m building Minispace.

With Minispace, you're inspired by bits and pieces of the web, to learn, to contribute, to read, to digest, to create, to foster, to build, to own, to nuture and to grow.


**Our Philosophy**

Small sites over massive platforms
Personal expression over performance metrics
Writing for joy, not algorithms
Minimalism, simplicity, and creative freedom
Encouraing people to take ownership of their own space on the web


**Not a techie?**

That's alright, you don't have to be. Learn to create your own experience. Be inspired by others, copy, refine, until you can call it yours.
