A Digital Garden is a tool used by people to keep track and manage all their readings, learnings and writings digitally.

You can choose to build your digital garden from scratch or build it using providers such as wordpress, bearblog, hugo, fomadocs etc.

Like a physical home garden, a digital garden is ever evolving. The seeds you plant grow, and this all happens because you tend to them.
You plant a seed (an idea) -> Seedling
You revisit and update that idea with new findings -> Budding
When that idea becomes a full on essay that you are proud of -> Evergreen
Being evergreen doesn't mean that the idea will no longer be updated. You keep updating as you find new quotes, facts or claims to back up your essay. Unlike regular blogs where an essay if forgotten after it is published, in a digital garden you keep updating your essay.

The beauty about keeping a digital garden is how you are able to draw connections between different ideas, notes, and essays.
