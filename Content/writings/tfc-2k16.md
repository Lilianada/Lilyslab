One thing about teenage girls is they possess so many clothing and accessories, and then when it's time to hang out with friends or go on a date, they suddenly have nothing to put on.

For the boys, they just go with whatever they find in their wardrobe; only some of them actually take their time "to look it."

I decided to put on a little photo shoot to display the clothing that trended during the summer. Also, combining the clothes in your wardrobe to get something *slay*-*ish* to put on. I titled it ***Teens Fashion Combo 2K16 (TFC 2K16)***

So for those who have been asking what TFC is all about, this is it. [P.S. The idea is & was solely mine]
