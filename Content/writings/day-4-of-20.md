You’ve probably heard this before ↴

"Don’t wait to give yourself permission to be happy."

But how many times have you thought:
— "I’ll be happy when I land that job."
— "I’ll relax after I the product launches.”
— "I’ll enjoy life once I make enough money."

And then, when you finally get there… you just set another goal.
The cycle repeats.

You keep chasing happiness like a moving target.

— Here’s the shift:
Fall in love with the process, not just the outcome.

When you enjoy the journey, success is no longer the condition for happiness—it becomes a byproduct of it.
