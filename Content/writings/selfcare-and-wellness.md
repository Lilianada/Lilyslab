Wellness goes beyond eating well and exercising. It is the conversations we have, the relationships we’re in, the people we let into our space, the books we read, our creative hobbies, the job we have, and basically everything that feeds our body, soul, and spirit. Wellness isn’t feeding your body with nutritional pills or dieting, it’s more than using energy stones. It is about staying committed to basic activities day in and day out that would have a great impact on your health.

Intentionally putting aside time to take care of ourselves physically, emotionally, mentally, and spiritually is essential to our well-being. Switching up your routine is part of self-care; you’re probably burned out and tired because you keep carrying out the same routine of things daily. A little change here and there will live a huge impact. 

Create quality time for yourself daily and try out new things outside of what your normal day looks like. These activities will help nourish your spirit, mind, and body. 

Prioritizing self-care is vital before extending care to others. To achieve overall well-being, it's essential to focus on the six dimensions of health: 
- physical, 
- emotional, 
- spiritual, 
- cognitive, 
- social, and 
- environmental.

Self-care is crucial in caring for ourselves in all these areas. Incorporate self-care into daily routines to make small changes that can have a significant impact. Small changes in daily routines can break monotony and have a significant impact on our well-being.
