This is how I read and this is how I work.
Why read one book at a time?
Why work on only one project at a time.

Reading 2-3 books together and making connections binding what you learn together.

Building 3-4 projects at a time, when you get bored or tired of one, you hop unto the other.

Yesterday I read a book that taught me project hopping, basically giving name to something I already do but thought was bad (Steal Like an Artist)

Today I read an essay called [on building nowledge networks through wandering](https://thecreativeindependent.com/essays/on-building-knowledge-networks-through-wandering/) that taught me co-reading, giving name to something I already do.

I somehow connected both ideas to form my own personal abstraction.
