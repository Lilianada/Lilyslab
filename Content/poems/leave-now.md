The best time to leave 
is the first time 
you thought of leaving.
The longer you stay, 
the harder it gets.

Time passes so fast, 
by the time the earth 
rotates fully round the sun, 
you'd be biting your tongue
and gnashing your teeth 
that you didn't leave 
the first time. 

If you had, 
fleeting memories,
deleted pictures 
cleared chats and
healing wounds.

But here you are,
regreting and wishing.
pain and regrets,
carving crevices in your soul.
The pain you feel
is the commeuppance for your staying.

The second best time
is now.
don't plan, 
just leave.
