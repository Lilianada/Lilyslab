now you're leaving in your answered prayers
but you're too small minded to notice it
you keep stressing over the things you can't control
instead of enjoying what's right here in front of you
you feel tired from the job you prayed for
overwhelmed by the growth you longed for
challenged by the life you created
exhausted by the challenge you chose
take a step back
and see what you're now priviledged to have
embrace it
enjoy it
else you'll lose it
an all you'll be left with are regrets
