if life is a choice,
how can we make the right choices?
if life is time,
how can we spend our time wisely?
if life is love,
how can we love whole heartedly?
if life is memory,
how can we make lasting memories?
if life is communication,
how can we communicate well?
if life is just life,
then it has no meaning.

once you come to this realization,
you can give it any meaning you want
twist and turn it
create your own meaning
not the meaning defined by society
nor the meaning assigned by parents
but a meaning from your own lenses.

time has a limit
live in the present but with te future in mind
live life like there is an end
make memories
make mistakes, 
but never make the same one twice.
keep in touch with friends
share the pizza
break bread
live life.
