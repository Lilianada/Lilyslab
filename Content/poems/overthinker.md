I spend so much time overthinking, 
comparing, 
waiting for life to feel more put together 
without realizing that this is it.

These slow mornings,
these quiet evenings, 
these random weekends,
the version of me who's still figuring it out. 

One day, I'll miss this.
I'll miss the freedom I didn't know I had, 
the strength I was building without noticing, 
the little joys I was too tired to celebrate.

Life isn't on pause, it's happening now.
Even in the chaos, 
even in the waiting, 
and maybe it's not the life I imagined yet, 
but it's still mine.
I'm still becoming, and that counts for something.
