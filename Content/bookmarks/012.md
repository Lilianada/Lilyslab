#### What are the storytelling opportunities in the work I'm already doing?
How can I create visually interesting, story-rich documentation of my process?
Think of every post, idea, and image as a seed. Once planted in others’ minds, what could it grow into?



#### What will make digital gardening enjoyable, meaningful, and valuable for me as a person? 

- Trying out building mini projects
- Giving life updates
- Adding new features to my garden

#### Who do I want to bring into my digital garden, and how can I invite them in?

- Recruiters
- Fellow industry collegues


#### What does it feel like to walk through my digital garden? 

Make a word cloud of impressions/main ideas a visitor might take away from a visit to your digital spaces.


#### What do you gain from visiting my digital garden?

- New ideas and insights
- Opportunities to learn something new
- Reading suggestions; book recommendations, articles to learn from, 
- Visual stimulation or an inspiring aesthetic; design and web inspiration
- Tools; tools others have built and the ones i have built to make you more productive
